# Domain Rupture Crater Design

## Goal

Make the shrine crater at `900 86 -360` look less neat and more like a cursed impact wound. The walls should be fully covered and the crater should become jagged, uneven, and creative while keeping the shrine portal usable.

## Visual Direction

- Cover all four crater walls with layered blackstone, deepslate, basalt, soul soil, magma, crying obsidian, purple glass, and crimson vein accents.
- Break the clean rectangular edges with uneven shelves, jutting ledges, rubble piles, diagonal-feeling scars, and height variation.
- Add more vertical chaos: basalt teeth, blackstone wall spikes, hanging chains, bone ribs embedded in the walls, and dripstone.
- Add cursed erosion pockets with purple glow, soul fire, magma cracks, and red organic blocks.
- Preserve the shrine crown, bone ribs, portal-mouth center, and entrance path.

## Functional Requirements

- Preserve entrance/view coordinates at `900 84 -392`.
- Preserve portal center at `900 82 -360` and verify it is still `nether_portal`.
- Keep the shrine playable and visible at night.
- Save the world after the roughening pass.
