# Compact FlameFrags Base Replica Design

## Goal

Create a smaller, recognizable exterior replica of the new FlameFrags base shown in the referenced YouTube Short. The build should capture the original mega-base's dark industrial circular architecture without reproducing its enormous scale.

## Scope

- Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.4 Overworld build.
- Approximate footprint: 90 by 90 blocks.
- One dominant central circular tower.
- Three smaller ring-shaped satellite towers.
- Elevated bridges connecting the towers.
- Compact, usable interior rooms rather than a full recreation of every facility shown in the video.
- Codex will scout and select a clear location that does not overlap existing builds.

## Visual Design

- Primary palette: deepslate bricks and tiles, polished blackstone, stone, and tuff.
- Structural highlights: light gray blocks and iron details to suggest metal ribs and machinery.
- Accent palette: dark red, copper, lava, and warm concealed lighting.
- Repeated rings, buttresses, vertical ribs, inset windows, and stepped roofs will create the recognizable fortress silhouette.
- Terrain work will blend the foundation into the site so the complex does not appear to float.

## Interior

- Central tower: entrance hall and compact storage/work area.
- One satellite: enchanting room.
- One satellite: brewing room.
- One satellite: compact farm or utility room.
- Bridges and rooms will be lit, enclosed, and safely traversable.

## Construction Method

- Scout candidate Overworld locations before selecting the build origin.
- Generate a datapack containing staged build functions.
- Construct the foundation, tower shells, bridges, roofs, details, and interiors in bounded batches to avoid server stalls.
- Preserve nearby terrain outside the selected footprint.

## Verification

- Confirm all staged functions complete without command errors.
- Inspect the build from several exterior viewpoints.
- Confirm all towers and bridges are connected and traversable.
- Confirm rooms are lit and hostile mobs cannot spawn inside.
- Save the world after construction and provide arrival coordinates.
